Inside

Light oak, stone and soft daylight

Light oak flooring in the living areas, passage and bedrooms; neutral stone in the kitchen, bathrooms and balcony; plastered walls. Sun, sky and glass are lit physically, with ceiling downlights and bedside lamps for the evening.

How it is made

Plan first, finish last

The house is modelled in Blender from one set of plan data, so the drawing, the model and the checks always agree. Textures, sky and a handful of accent models are free CC0 assets from Poly Haven. The rest is built in code.

  1. Plan

    One source of truth

    Rooms, walls and openings in a single file feed the drawing and the 3D model.

  2. Structure

    Walls and openings

    Butt-jointed walls, floors, doors, windows and the balcony, checked against the plan.

  3. Furnish

    Finishes and furniture

    Real textures, furniture kept clear of doors and walking routes, daylight and downlights.

  4. Shots

    21 views

    Exterior, top-down, cut-away and every room, rendered in Cycles.

  5. Film

    Walkthrough

    One eased move at eye level from the entry to the master bedroom.
